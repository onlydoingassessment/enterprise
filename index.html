GonkRewards

Customer Loyalty set up in seconds



What is GonkRewards?

GonkRewards is a solution that has been found for all buisnesses that need to retain their customers. It is a portable solution that allows even small buisnesses to have their own loyalty rewards program, and can be applied at any scale.


Gain important data from customers

GonkRewards empowers you to collect data from your customers and get a better view on your customer base, so your buisness can adapt in the right way.


How is GonkRewards used?

To begin, you will need to sign your buisness up to GonkRewards and complete the initialization steps. You can then set the ways customers are rewarded with points and the offers they get once they reach certain point values. You can also set things such as surveys to collect data from your customers in the program in exchange for points.


What is the cost?

GonkRewards itself is free, however you will need to set rewards such as products and discounts to be given once your customers reach certain thresholds of points and similar.
